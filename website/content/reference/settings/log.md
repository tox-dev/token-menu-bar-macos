**Detailed logging** starts off. Below it, a bounded-height viewer with level and text filters is always visible, with
**Copy**, **Clear** and **Show Full Log**. The full-log window exposes the retained log in a larger searchable view.

Detailed logs include refresh timings, panel geometry, tab measurements and status-width decisions.
[Diagnostics guidance](/troubleshooting/diagnostics/) explains what to collect and what to redact.
