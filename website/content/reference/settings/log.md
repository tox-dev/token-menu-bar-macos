**Detailed logging** starts off and shares a row with **Copy**, **Clear** and **Show Full Log**. Below them, a
bounded-height viewer with level and text filters is always visible. The full-log window exposes the retained log in a
larger searchable view.

Detailed logs include refresh timings, panel geometry, tab measurements and status-width decisions.
[Diagnostics guidance](/troubleshooting/diagnostics/) explains what to collect and what to redact.
