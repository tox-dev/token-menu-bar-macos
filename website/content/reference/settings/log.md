**Detailed logging** starts off. **View log** opens a bounded-height viewer with level and text filters, **Copy**,
**Clear** and **Show Full Log**. The full-log window exposes the retained log in a larger searchable view. Closing the
disclosure does not turn logging off.

Detailed logs include refresh timings, panel geometry, tab measurements and status-width decisions.
[Diagnostics guidance](/troubleshooting/diagnostics/) explains what to collect and what to redact.
