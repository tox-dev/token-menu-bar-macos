---
title: Notifications settings
description: Thresholds, reset alerts, pace and delivery.
weight: 5
---

The **Notifications** master switch hides subordinate controls when off and preserves their values. Alerts start on,
with 75%, 90% and 100% thresholds; 50% is available too. Other controls cover window resets, sign-in needs, pace, sound
and expiring Codex reset credits where applicable.

Threshold crossings in one refresh group into a notice. Pace alerts suppress the first 5% of a period and avoid
repeating within that period unless the pace recovers first. Delivery also depends on macOS permissions and Focus.
